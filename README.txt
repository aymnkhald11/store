OUTFIT STORE  -  by AY CODE
Open index.html in the browser.

Put your photos in the images folder:
  images/hero.jpg      (home page big background)
  images/stats.jpg     (numbers section background)
  images/man1.jpg, man2.jpg, man3.jpg   (slider next to "Why choose us")
  images/products/     (product photos, names are in main.js -> PRODUCTS)
If a photo is missing the site shows a colored placeholder, nothing breaks.

Edit products, prices and shipping at the top of main.js.
Orders are saved in the browser (localStorage). Real card payment needs a payment gateway.
